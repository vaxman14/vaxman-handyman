Accessibility Statement

Last updated: June 2, 2026

Vaxman Handyman Services is committed to ensuring our website is accessible to all users, including those with disabilities. We strive to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA standards.

Our Commitment

We believe everyone deserves equal access to information about our services. We are continuously working to improve the accessibility of our website and user experience for all visitors.

Accessibility Features

Our website includes the following built-in accessibility features, available via the ♿ widget in the lower-left corner of every page:

  • High Contrast Mode — Increases color contrast for users with low vision.
  • Reduced Motion — Disables animations and transitions for users with vestibular disorders.
  • Dyslexia-Friendly Font — Switches to a font designed to improve readability for users with dyslexia.
  • Text Size Adjustment — Allows users to increase or decrease base font size.

All accessibility preferences are saved locally in your browser and persist across sessions.

Additional Measures

  • Semantic HTML structure with proper heading hierarchy
  • Descriptive alt text for images
  • Keyboard navigable interface
  • ARIA labels on interactive elements
  • Sufficient color contrast ratios throughout
  • Responsive design for all screen sizes

Known Limitations

While we strive for full accessibility, some older content or third-party embeds may not fully meet WCAG 2.1 AA guidelines. We are actively working to address any gaps.

Feedback & Assistance

If you experience any difficulty accessing content on our website, or if you need information in an alternative format, please contact us. We welcome your feedback and will work to address your needs promptly.

Email: info@vaxmanhandyman.com

We aim to respond to accessibility inquiries within 2 business days.

Standards Reference
