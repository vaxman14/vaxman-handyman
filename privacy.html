Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2, 2026

Vaxman Handyman Services ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal information when you visit our website or request our services.

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Contact information — name, email address, phone number, and address when you submit a quote request.

How We Use Your Information

Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information with:

We retain your information only as long as necessary to provide services and comply with legal obligations. You may request deletion of your data at any time.

Your Rights (California Residents — CCPA)

As a California resident, you have the right to:

  • Non-discrimination for exercising your rights

To exercise these rights, contact us at the email below.
