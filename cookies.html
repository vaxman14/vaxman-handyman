Cookie Policy

Last updated: June 2, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Vaxman Handyman Services uses cookies and similar tracking technologies on our website.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your web browser. They help websites remember your preferences and improve your experience over time.

Types of Cookies We Use

Essential Cookies

These cookies are required for the website to function and cannot be disabled. They do not store personally identifiable information.

  • cookies_accepted — Remembers your cookie consent choice
  • ada_* — Stores your accessibility preferences (text size, contrast, motion settings)

Functional Cookies

These cookies enhance website functionality and personalization. They may be set by us or by third-party services.

  • Form pre-fill data for quote requests
  • Session identifiers for smooth navigation

Analytics Cookies

If analytics tools are enabled, these cookies collect anonymous data about how visitors use our site (pages visited, time on site, etc.) to help us improve. They do not identify you personally.

Your Choices

When you first visit our site, a cookie banner gives you the option to accept or decline non-essential cookies. You can change your preferences at any time by:

  • Clearing cookies in your browser settings
  • Using your browser's private/incognito mode
  • Adjusting browser cookie settings to block third-party cookies

Note: disabling essential cookies may affect website functionality.

Third-Party Cookies

We may use third-party services (such as form processors or analytics providers) that set their own cookies. These are governed by their own privacy policies.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
